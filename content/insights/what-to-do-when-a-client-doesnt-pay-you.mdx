## Start with the paper trail

Before anything else, gather what you have: the signed agreement or accepted
quote, the invoice, proof of delivery, and the correspondence around it. A
recovery process moves much faster when the underlying record is complete.

## Escalate deliberately

A structured escalation — a reminder, then a formal demand, then a letter of
demand from your attorney — often resolves the matter without litigation. Each
step signals seriousness while leaving room for the relationship to survive.

## Know when to move

If the debtor is unresponsive or disputing in bad faith, waiting costs you more
than acting. Debt recovery and commercial dispute processes exist precisely for
this point.
