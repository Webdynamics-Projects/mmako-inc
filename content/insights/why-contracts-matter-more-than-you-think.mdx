## The contract is the plan

Most commercial relationships run smoothly until an assumption turns out to be
unshared. A well-drafted agreement writes those assumptions down while everyone
is still on good terms.

## Where template agreements fall short

Generic templates rarely reflect how your business actually operates — payment
timing, scope changes, liability, or what happens when either side wants out.
Those are exactly the clauses that get tested.

## Speed and protection aren't opposites

A contract can protect your position without slowing the deal down. The goal is
clarity, not length.
