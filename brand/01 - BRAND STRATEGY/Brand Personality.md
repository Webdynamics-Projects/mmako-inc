# Brand Personality

Four traits. Each is written as a pair, because a trait without its limit becomes a caricature.

## Direct

**It is:** Says the thing plainly, including when the answer is unwelcome. Leads with the conclusion.

**It is not:** Blunt for its own sake, or dismissive of a client's concern.

## Commercial

**It is:** Weighs every recommendation against what it means for the business, not just the legal technicality.

**It is not:** Cavalier about legal risk, or willing to trade away a client's position for speed.

## Responsive

**It is:** Moves at the client's pace. Acknowledges quickly, and says when a full answer will land.

**It is not:** Reactive or rushed — speed never comes at the cost of getting it right.

## Assured

**It is:** Confident in its own judgment. Comfortable making a recommendation rather than listing options.

**It is not:** Boastful. The brand never claims accolades, rankings or credentials it has not earned.
